Swerve's Bar PODCASTS ABOUT CONTACT CASTING CALL

Fun Script Read

Details

We made a Script for TFCon LA but it never made it to the table. Thus we are doing a volunteer-based Casting Call so that the story may live on!

We will be reaching out to those who are chosen the weekend of November 21st, 2026 with more information.

Once the project is finished it will be posted on our Youtube/Podcast/and shown off at furture Conventions!

Rules

  • Record 2-3 takes of all provided Try-Out Dialogues, in a quiet room with minimal background noise.
  • Each recording should be recorded as following : (Example : Beast Wars Megatron, Take 1 line 1 ..., Take 2 line 1..., Take 3 line 1..., Take 1 Line 2 ... and so on.)
  • Save your files in the following format : CharacterName_YourName.mp3
  • Keep all your takes of a single character to one file please. You may apply for any number of roles and for any character regardless of gender of that character.
  • Send your entries to Swervesbarpodcast@gmail.com with the subject line "Script Read '26".
  • Please no spamming our email with multiple takes of the same character, we will only look at the first entry you send per character.

    • *Character Line Count are Subject to change*
